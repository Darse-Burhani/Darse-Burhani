# Product Context: Darse Burhani

## 1. Overview & Institutional Purpose
Darse Burhani is the comprehensive academic, administrative, and biometric portal for Mahad al Zahra. It manages attendance, class schedules, substitute teaching allocations (badal), student achievements, takhteet curriculum tracking, library services, and notifications for faculty, students (talabat), administrators, and parents.

## 2. User Roles & Personas
- **Admin:** Operations directors managing biometric terminal telemetry, class registrations, attendance schedules, faculty duty allocations, and institutional reporting.
- **Teacher (Faculty):** Instructors managing daily roll-calls, period timetables, takhteet curriculum planning, leave applications, and student progress reports.
- **Talabat (Students):** Students reviewing their daily attendance, period schedule, homework assignments, achievements, and tilawat-dua progress.
- **Parent:** Guardians monitoring their children's real-time attendance, leave statuses, and institutional announcements.

## 3. Core Operational Modes & Surfaces
- **Mode:** `Operate` — high scanability, rapid task completion, native mobile ergonomics, instant search, and clear visual hierarchy.
- **Brand Identity:** Fatimi architectural aesthetic: 70% warm cream / pure white surfaces, 20% deep espresso typography, and 10% antique burnished gold singular accents.
- **Mobile First:** Optimized for handheld usage, PWA offline capabilities, 44px minimum tap targets, thumb-accessible bottom dock, and responsive collapse.
